Spellbook

Swap two neighbouring runes to line up 3 or more of the same element. You can keep swapping while cascades are still falling — only moving runes are locked.

🔥 Match 4 → Flame Rune: explodes 3×3.

✴ L or T shape → Star Rune: blasts its whole row and column.

🔮 Match 5 in a line → Arcane Orb: swap it with any rune to strike every rune of that colour with lightning.

☀ Match 6+ → Supernova: three rows and three columns.

💥 Swap two power-ups together for bigger spells. Orb + Orb clears the board!

Chains build a cascade multiplier. Fill the mana bar to level up.

Touch: swipe a rune, or tap two. Mouse: drag or click. Xbox: D-pad/stick moves, hold or tap A then push a direction to swap, B cancels, Y hint, Start pause.
